    05 / PRIVACY & LIMITSTRANSPARENT

    Privacy & limitations

    • Exit detection uses ipwho.is with Cloudflare as a fallback, every two minutes and on network changes. Country alone does not prove a VPN is connected.
    • Manual IPv4 / IPv6 checks use ipify. Exit observations and settings stay in local extension storage.
    • Reputation reports send the IP to AbuseIPDB or Spamhaus only when you open a source link. Browsing history is not sent. No internal score or safety guarantee is provided.
    • WebRTC policy applies browser-wide and may disrupt calls. Turning protection off releases this extension-controlled setting.
    • RaptorShield does not create a VPN or affect other apps. Split tunneling may give different sites different routes.
    • Module workers and some newer execution contexts are not fully covered. Leak protection, IP reputation and anonymity are not guaranteed.
